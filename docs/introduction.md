## Project context

The supplied project brief associates this work with WP3, “Intersectoral mobility for R&I translation towards ethically responsible innovations”, and T3.1, “ART staff upskilling towards the improvement of quality and equity in access to fertility treatment”.

The guidelines are intended to support identifying translation challenges, designing processes to address them, improving translation of NESTOR results and increasing applied research capacity. Source verification is tracked in [References](references.md).

## Scope and boundaries

The five domains are personalised reproductive medicine, assisted reproductive technologies, non-invasive prenatal testing, uterine health, and AI/data-driven solutions for ART.

Clinical validation and translation are the central purpose. Data management, interoperability, harmonisation, privacy and data quality are supporting considerations where relevant.

Research/software implementation remains in separate repositories, including `embryo-vision`. Future reviewed case studies may link to specific implementation versions while the framework here remains reusable across domains.

## Intended audience and use

The intended audience includes NESTOR researchers, ART staff and collaborators developing or assessing innovations for translation toward clinical practice.

Start with the [validation framework](validation-framework/index.md), then consult the relevant [domain protocols](protocols/index.md) and [templates](templates/index.md) as they become available.
