## Scientific and methodological references

### NICE — Evidence standards framework for digital health technologies

National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE). *Evidence standards framework for digital health technologies*, ECD7. [How to meet the standards](https://www.nice.org.uk/corporate/ecd7/chapter/how-to-meet-the-standards), standards 10–13. Accessed 28 September 2026.

Used in [clinical problem and rationale](validation-framework/index.md#topic-selection-and-clinical-problem) to support describing the population, existing pathway, proposed changes and expected benefits. This is a UK digital-health assessment framework, not a blanket requirement for this EU project. The linked online guidance may change over time.

### IMDRF — Software as a Medical Device: Clinical Evaluation

International Medical Device Regulators Forum, Software as a Medical Device Working Group. *Software as a Medical Device (SaMD): Clinical Evaluation*. IMDRF/SaMD WG/N41FINAL:2017, 21 September 2017. [Official document (PDF)](https://www.imdrf.org/sites/default/files/docs/imdrf/final/technical/imdrf-tech-170921-samd-n41-clinical-evaluation_1.pdf), sections 4–7. Accessed 28 September 2026.

Used in [intended use](validation-framework/index.md#intended-use) to distinguish clinical association, technical validation and clinical validation. Its scope is SaMD; it is not a determination that any NESTOR method is a medical device. The document describes harmonised principles rather than regulations.
