# Validation checklist

Use this working SOP when planning and reporting a validation study. **(M)** marks items for multi-site studies; **(C)** marks items for work moving towards clinical use. Record a reason where an item does not apply. A completed checklist documents the study; it does not establish clinical readiness.

[Download the editable checklist](downloads/NESTOR_D3.3_Validation_Checklist_v3.docx). On this page, selections last until you reload or leave it. Use your browser’s Print command to retain checked items on paper or as a PDF.

## 1. Task definition

[Workflow guidance](practical-workflow.md#section-5-1) · [Regulatory checkpoints](regulatory-guidance.md#table-4) · [Harmonised minimum practices](harmonisation.md#table-5)

- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Research question and expected outputs stated.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Intended purpose, intended user and target population written down.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Acceptable and unacceptable errors defined with clinical input.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Responsibilities for review and the role of human judgement defined.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Regulatory qualification assessed (medical device / IVD / research only). (C)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Ethics review requirement and DPIA screening completed.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Task definition and acceptance criteria agreed by all sites. (M)</label>

## 2. Data description

[Workflow guidance](practical-workflow.md#section-5-2) · [Regulatory checkpoints](regulatory-guidance.md#table-4) · [Harmonised minimum practices](harmonisation.md#table-5)

- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Data sources, collection and preparation described.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Lawful basis, consent or opt-out status and ethics approval recorded for each site.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Data processing or joint-controllership agreements in place. (M)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Processing location recorded; model-to-data approach used where transfer is restricted.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Harmonised acquisition metadata and data dictionary applied. (M)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Reference standard and annotation protocol documented; inter-observer agreement measured.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Training and evaluation data split at patient or cycle level; external site held out. (M)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Inclusion and exclusion criteria documented; representativeness and gaps described.</label>

## 3. Model identification

[Workflow guidance](practical-workflow.md#section-5-3) · [Regulatory checkpoints](regulatory-guidance.md#table-4) · [Harmonised minimum practices](harmonisation.md#table-5)

- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Model version, weights checksum, configuration and input preparation recorded.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Meaning of output labels, values and units described.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Same model version verified at every site. (M)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Ownership and licensing of model and data recorded.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Version control and change records maintained. (C)</label>

## 4. Evaluation and visual review

[Workflow guidance](practical-workflow.md#section-5-4) · [Regulatory checkpoints](regulatory-guidance.md#table-4) · [Harmonised minimum practices](harmonisation.md#table-5)

- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Performance measures and unit of analysis pre-specified.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Quality-review criteria pre-specified at frame and sequence or participant level.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Exclusions recorded with coded reasons; exclusion rates reported.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Results reported per site and overall, with confidence intervals. (M)</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Human oversight arrangements and identified failure modes documented. (C)</label>

## 5. Documentation and interpretation

[Workflow guidance](practical-workflow.md#section-5-5) · [Regulatory checkpoints](regulatory-guidance.md#table-4) · [Harmonised minimum practices](harmonisation.md#table-5)

- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Findings traceable to data, model version and review decisions.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Output quality and derived measurements reported separately.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Reporting guideline followed (TRIPOD+AI, CLAIM or DECIDE-AI).</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Stage of evidence stated; no unsupported claims of clinical benefit.</label>
- <label class="checklist-item"><input type="checkbox"> Records archived according to the data management plan.</label>

## Deviations and follow-up

Record the item, reason for any deviation or non-applicability, responsible person and follow-up date in the study record. Store completed records in the institution’s approved environment.
