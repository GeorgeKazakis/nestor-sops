## Protocols and SOPs serve different purposes

A validation protocol describes how a particular innovation will be evaluated. An SOP describes how to carry out a specific, repeatable activity within that work. One protocol may reference several SOPs.

The shared [validation protocol template](../templates/validation-protocol-template.md) and [SOP template](../templates/sop-template.md) are available as drafts for editorial review across all five domains. They are document structures, not completed study protocols or operational procedures. Detailed domain procedures will follow separately.
